Implementation Protocol

Strict Spatial Discipline for Warehouse Clients

In high-frequency pick-pack operations, every millisecond counts. Buttons maintain consistent 44px touch targets on mobile terminals, tables keep row heights locked to 48px to prevent touch mis-hits, and high-contrast status colors immediately signal operational roadblocks across dock staging zones.
